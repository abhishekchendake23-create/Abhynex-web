      ABOUT THE AUTHOR · ABX CREATOR

      Abhishek Sanjay Chendake

      AUTHOR • AI CREATOR • ENGINEERING STUDENT • DIGITAL CREATOR

      Abhishek Chendake is an independent author and writer from Mulegaon, Solapur, Maharashtra, India. He explores philosophy, self-discovery, human thought, existence and personal growth through his writing.

      His interests connect writing, Artificial Intelligence, engineering, technology, creative thinking and continuous learning. Through books and digital projects, he explores ideas, knowledge and technology while building his own path in the digital world.

      He is deeply curious about life, human nature, emotions and existence. He believes every individual has a unique story, and that writing is a way of thinking, a way to explore ideas and emotions.

      His parents, Sanjay Chendake and Kavita Chendake, shaped his values and personality.

      Through his books he aims to create moments of reflection: moments that make readers pause, question their beliefs and see things from a new perspective.
